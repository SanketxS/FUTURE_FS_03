Our story

Built by baristas, loved by neighbors.

We started as a weekend pop-up, sharing micro-lot beans with friends. Today, Copper Cup is a bright corner café where slow coffee meets fast hospitality. We source transparently, dial in every roast, and bake each pastry before sunrise.
